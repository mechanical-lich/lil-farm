# Task Balancing Theory

In a game like there are two types of tasks.   Fast and Slow.   Fast tasks are what keeps a player actively playing.  Slow keeps a player checking in.  We need a balance of both.   

My thoughts: 
- Tasks like planting and watering crops are fast tasks that the player can watch happen and complete in a play session.
- Tasks like building a fence, chopping trees, etc as slower and can be queued to be completed while the player is offline.  They then check their game later to find that the fence has been completed by the farmer while they were away.
- While not a direct task itself, waiting for crops to grow in ways counts as a slow task as well.
